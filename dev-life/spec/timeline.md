# Dev Life — the timeline

Part of [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md). The seven chapters, their goals and the history events by year. The data lives in `data/eras.js`; the market's numbers in `data/market.js` ([career.md › The market](career.md#the-market)).

Rules for history:

- **Up to 2026 the events are real** and kept general: the trade's big turns, not company dramas or people's names. Product and technology names are allowed where they are part of the trade's history (smartphones, Docker, React, ChatGPT); they are named in the event's text, never used as branding of the game.
- **From 2027 the future is invented** and every such card carries a small *forecast* tag; it should feel plausible and stay optimistic in tone (the game is calm).
- A history event changes the market, the prices, the job offers' shapes or the options (remote work, AI tools) — and comes as one card with a picture and a line or two; most have no choice, some have one. In the student years the history cards are fewer and lighter (they show up as the IT club's talks and the news Max reads aloud), but the market still moves: it decides which internships and first jobs come.

## Chapter 1 — Hello, World (2010-09 → 2014-06)

Age 18–22. The dorm with Max → maybe a rented room with friends. The old laptop → the first PC. All of it in [university.md](university.md).

- **Goals:** career *Graduate with a job offer in hand* · health *Keep Rest above 50 through every exam session* · life *Build your first PC*.
- **Scripted:** the welcome week and the clubs (Sep 2010), the first exam session (Jan 2011) and the first stipend decision, the first summer, the course projects, the 3rd year's electives and the exchange semester's chance, the internship (summer 2013), the part-time junior job's chance, the thesis, the defence and the graduation (June 2014), the master's card.
- **History:** 2010 smartphones everywhere, app stores boom (Mobile demand ▲; the Mobile elective popular); 2011 SSDs become affordable (the first-SSD lesson on the old laptop), the famous unlockable 4-core CPU generation — the first PC's heart; 2012 the cloud grows (DevOps & Cloud ▲), single-page web apps; 2013 containers appear (Docker), React is open-sourced — the IT club's talks; 2014 a new language for iOS, ML starts its rise (Data & AI ▲).

## Chapter 2 — Stack Overflow (2014-07 → 2016)

Age 22–24. The first rented flat. The first own PC on the desk.

- **Goals:** career *Become Middle* · health *Find a sport you keep for a year* · life *Go to your first conference*.
- **Scripted:** the first full-time job (or the job search), Dana as a mentor, the first salary and the first rent, moving out of the dorm or the shared room, Max's birthday, the first raise talk, the master's in the evenings if taken.
- **History:** 2014 16 GB becomes the developer's norm; 2015 containers go mainstream, the web's frameworks churn ("framework fatigue" card: Spark − if the hero chases every new one); 2016 deep learning beats a Go champion (Data & AI ▲, GPUs matter for learning).

## Chapter 3 — Merge Request (2017 → 2019)

Age 25–27. A better flat; maybe abroad.

- **Goals:** career *Become Senior* · health *No burnout through the chapter* · life *Move in together* (or, without a partner, *A home of your own*).
- **Scripted:** Max's startup offer (or in chapter 5), a relocation offer abroad, rent or buy.
- **History:** 2017 the many-core CPU war (cheap 8 cores), crypto mining makes GPUs expensive (the mining card, [computer.md › Mining](computer.md#mining-20172018)); 2018 data-privacy rules come to Europe (Security ▲, a work card), the crypto crash; 2019 Kubernetes everywhere (DevOps ▲▲).

## Chapter 4 — Work From Home (2020 → 2022)

Age 28–30. The home office becomes the workplace.

- **Goals:** career *Thrive remote: two good reviews while remote* · health *Keep Fitness above 50 through the lockdowns* · life *A home office with a door* (or *A wedding*, if together).
- **Scripted:** the wedding (if together; a small one in the lockdown or a big one later), the home office upgrade, a pet.
- **History:** 2020-03 **the pandemic**: offices close, all jobs remote for months, Friends decays faster, gyms closed (sport → running and home workouts), the desk becomes vital (webcam, headset, chair cards); a three-month hiring dip, then a remote-hiring boom; 2020-11 ARM laptops arrive (a Codex card); 2021 **the chip shortage**: GPUs and consoles out of stock, a waiting list, prices up; 2022 offices reopen as hybrid (a choice per job), the late-2022 **layoffs** start, 2022-11-30 **ChatGPT** — the closing card of the chapter: *"Something new arrived. Everyone is talking about it."* (AI tools skill opens).

## Chapter 5 — The AI Era (2023 → 2027)

Age 31–35. The trade changes faster than ever.

- **Goals:** career *Reach AI tools 5 and keep a job you like* · health *A check-up every year and act on it* · life *A first child* (or another Life Dream of the *life* kind chosen by the player: *An own home*, *Live abroad*).
- **Scripted:** layoffs at the hero's company (a card: *stay and take more work*, *take the package*, *the company keeps you* by performance), the first child (a joint decision) and the parental leave choice, the lead / architect / manager branch, Max's startup offer if not taken in chapter 3.
- **History:** 2023 AI assistants in every editor (the AI tools multiplier at work; juniors' demand ▼; the *"Will AI take my job?"* card: Calm − unless AI tools ≥ 3, with the choice to learn it); 2024 AI demand ▲▲, GPUs for AI expensive, local models on a home GPU (VRAM matters); 2025 AI agents write and run code (*"Vibe coding"* card: output ▲ for those with AI tools, Fundamentals matters for checking the work); 2026 memory and GPU prices rise with AI data centres, the four-day-week debate; 2027 *forecast*: agent teams in companies — the hero's role shifts to reviewing, designing and leading (Leadership and Architecture value ▲).

## Chapter 6 — The Long Game (2028 → 2034)

Age 36–42. The invented future begins; the hero's own path.

- **Goals:** career *Your own path: CTO, founder, teacher, architect or part-time — reach its middle* · health *Ten years of a sport* (counted from any start) · life *Time for the kids: Kids ≥ 70 for a year* (or *Friends ≥ 70 for a year* without kids).
- **Scripted:** a second child (optional), a house with a garden, parents start to need help, the kids' school years begin.
- **History, all *forecast*:** 2028 the four-day week spreads in tech (a choice in new offers); 2029 home robots for chores (money buys time); 2030 AR glasses replace monitors for some (a desk option); 2031 programming in plain language is normal, the craft moves to systems, reliability and taste (Fundamentals and Architecture ▲); 2033 a big AI-safety rule set (Security ▲▲); 2034 personal AI tutors (learning ×1.5 for the hero and the kids).

## Chapter 7 — Legacy (2035 → the 50th birthday, autumn 2042)

Age 43–50.

- **Goals:** career *Reach the top of your path* · health *Fit at 50: Fitness ≥ 60 on the 50th birthday* · life *Build a PC with a young one*.
- **Scripted:** **the young one's first PC** — from 2035, the oldest child at 12 or older (without kids: a niece or nephew, a neighbour's kid, or a mentee from the hero's teaching), see [life.md › Kids](life.md#kids); the oldest leaves home (about 2040 if born in 2022); parents' late years (help cards, never a death); **the 50th birthday party** — a big card with everyone the hero kept in touch with (its guests are the result of 32 years of Friends and Family), then the Memoir.
- **History, all *forecast*:** 2036 the university of the hero invites them to teach a course (a card: Leadership +, Communication +, Spark +); 2037 green computing rules (the PSU's efficiency and the PC's power matter, a card); 2039 quiet, small, very fast home machines; 2042 the story's end.
