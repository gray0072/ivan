# Arlanda Rising — testing and balancing

Part of the spec of `arlanda-rising`; the core rules and the index of these files are in [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md). Keep it in sync with the code like the rest of the spec. The checkers, how to run them and the recipes that put the game into a state.

## Testing and balancing

- `sim/` is deterministic from a seed and has no DOM: a whole mission runs in Node; a mission saved and resumed at any second gives the same result as one run straight through (a test for the autosave).
- `tools/missions-check.js` runs every mission with its **reference strategy** (a scripted list of decisions in `data/strategies/`, played by `sim/strategy.js`) at every difficulty and reports the stars, the money curve, the meters and the minutes at ▶; it fails when the reference misses ★★★ on Easy or Medium, when doing nothing passes, when the previous chapter's strategy gets ★★★, when a "Real Arlanda" 🏅 is unreachable, or when a mission is longer than `MISSION_MINUTES_MAX`.
  - Run it after any change to `sim/`, `constants.js` or `data/`: `node arlanda-rising/tools/missions-check.js [mission id…] [--verbose]` (all of chapter 1 takes about two seconds; everything about half a minute). It also plays each Challenge Day (`c1`, `c2`, `c3`) from its own airport with its reference strategy: it fails when the reference misses ★★★ on Easy or Medium, when doing nothing gets ★★★, and when a resumed day ends differently. It plays the campaign as a chain — each mission starts from the airport the reference left after the one before, at the same difficulty — so a change early in a chapter moves the money of every later mission.
  - It also fails when a mission starts with more check-in desks than the halls have room for, more security lanes than the checkpoints, more passport booths than the passport hall or more remote stands than their row (the limits the buy menu shows, `Airport.cap`); when a goal about adding something (`built`, `fireCat`, `chargers`, `estate`, `runways`, `brands`, not one marked `keep`) is met at its mission's start (see "What the campaign opens" in [chapters.md](chapters.md)); when a reference strategy buys a small build its mission has not opened; and when a small build is in no category of `BUY_MENU` or in two, or a mission unlocks what is not a small build or what another mission unlocks. It also fails when the reference's cash goes below zero in a mission or it starts one on the owner's loan, at any difficulty (the money every stage needs is there, from the budgets, the earnings and the loans it takes); when the reference does not pass on Hard, when a stage is longer than `STAGE_MINUTES_MAX`, and when the mission played with a save, a throw-away and a resume in the middle of every stage (`Missions.save` / `Missions.resume`, as the autosave does) does not end in exactly the same state as one played straight through; and when the reference plays a dated stage (a stage's `date`, the first stage the mission's `date`) more than 3 days after its date, at any difficulty — a date only moves the calendar forward, so plan days that run past it (the mission's own, or the mission before's) would put "26 June 1960" or Christmas on a later day. The strategy of the mission before getting ★★★ too is a warning (`?`), not a failure: an exam reuses the chapter's moves.
  - `tools/terminals-check.js` (a second) checks the terminals through the years: each name in 1976, 1990, 1995, 2022 and 2030; Terminal 3 from the exam of 1989 with its units and cells, the propeller flights through it (and to the domestic terminal or Terminal 5 while it is mothballed); 7-1's choice mothballing Terminals 2, 3 and 4 and chapter 8 opening Terminal 3 again; the first terminal standing in 2017 and gone in 2018 with the kerb kept, its shops moved (and sold back when the other terminals are full), its units closed; Free Play and the Challenge Days with Terminal 3. Run it after changing `data/terminals.js`, a terminal's project or the missions' `requires`.
  - `tools/turnaround-check.js [mission id…] [--difficulty d]` plays the campaign with the reference strategies (about half a minute at Medium) and after every step checks each aircraft at a stand: the bar's four steps are done exactly when the sim would go on, the fuel stays empty while its vehicle drives (the truck's card says *driving to*) and fills only at the wing, the oil company's fuel progresses, the shares never go back, boarding's minutes are the sim's, a held-up step says why. It prints the ways seen per mission (fuel by truck, the oil company, robots, a stand's charger, the mobile charger, hydrogen; the bags by hand, de-icing, overnight, wide-bodies). Run it after changing `sim/turnaround.js`, the bar or the flight's card.
  - `--verbose` shows every stage's goals with their values, the stars and why doing nothing fails; `--money` the owner's budget, the cash, the debt and the loan room at each mission's start and end and its lowest cash, per difficulty (the budgets of [missions.md](missions.md)). To see one stage flight by flight, load the same files in a `vm` context (the list at the top of the checker) and print `S.ops.flights` at the stage's end: each flight keeps its planned and real times, its stand, its check-in and its turnaround's steps.
  - A stage, an option or a rule changed: run the checker, then tune the mission's goals and stars to the reference's numbers with a margin, so the reference keeps ★★★ and doing nothing fails.
- The Node checkers load the game's logic from one list, `tools/sim-files.js` (the files of `constants.js`, `data/` and `sim/` in the order `index.html` loads them, and a vm context with them): a new file of `sim/` or `data/` goes there and into `index.html`.
- `tools/freeplay-bot.js` (v0, step 1 of [freeplay.md](freeplay.md); a few seconds for 24 months) plays the non-stop Free Play from an era preset with its routes as they are, doing every tray task after `--react` game minutes (or none with `--notray`) and answering nothing, and prints per played month the day's flights, the tasks by hand / duty manager / teams, the real seconds at ▶ by pace (attention, cruise, idle day, night — from `FreePace.stepSeconds`, the browser's own function), the milliseconds of computing of the day and the night, and the cash. It fails when a game played again ends differently, when one saved and resumed in the middle of a played day or of a night ends differently, or a played day takes more than 3 s of computing. Run it after any change to `sim/free.js`, `sim/freepace.js`, `data/freeplay.js` or the `FREE_*` constants: `node arlanda-rising/tools/freeplay-bot.js [--era 1963] [--months 24] [--difficulty medium] [--react 2] [--notray] [--auto normal|gentle|off] [--quiet]`.
- `tools/layout-check.js` checks the map's geometry (see "Geography and layout" in [world.md](world.md): what may not stand on what, and the airside fence in its eras) and with `--svg` writes `docs/layout.svg` (the fence with everything built in green dashes); `--all` lists the allowed overlaps too. Run it after any change to `data/layout.js`, `render/fence.js`, a stand's place (`at`, `spots` in `data/masterplan.js` and `data/buildings.js`) or a project's plot, and regenerate the plan; then look at the map in the browser with `node --experimental-websocket tools/cdp.js arlanda-rising/tools/map-shots.js [out-dir]` (2040 in summer: the terminals, the north, Cargo City, the landside road, the player's pier and its satellite design with aircraft at its west-facing stands; 1976: the first terminal and the road's end). It knows nothing of the eras: a clash between two things of different years still counts, so give each its own ground.
- **Debugging is a big part of this game — make it cheap.** The map, the sim and the missions are large; a question like "why does this road cross that apron" or "why did this flight wait" will come again and again. So: every recurring check becomes a tool in `tools/` (kept, with a header saying what it checks and how to run it), every recurring picture a generated file in `docs/` (the plan), every recipe that puts the game into a state a line in this section; a new kind of thing on the map gets its shape added to `tools/layout-check.js` in the same change.
- Browser checks with `tools/cdp.js` (see `docs/testing.md`), short and one at a time; one check locks the page mid-stage (`visibilitychange`) and reloads it to see the mission continue. Recipes for a steps file (after `page('arlanda-rising')`):

  - The campaign played by the reference strategies through the game's own flow (a few seconds): for each mission `Game.startMission(id, d)`, then `Strategy.act(r); Sim.step()` in a loop (`Missions.next()` on `stageDone`) with `GameEvents.mute(true)`, then `Game.missionEnd()`.
  - The resume: `Game.save(); location.reload()`, then `Game.continueMission()` and compare `S.clock` and `S.money`.
  - The analytics tabs: a mission played by its reference strategy into an ops stage (2-11 to stage 2 at 09:30 has 100 plan days and a morning of quarter hours; 6-11 for the Green tab), `Game.play(); Game.speed = 0`, then `Game.openPanel('finance', 'staff')` (or a click on `.meter[data-m="staff"]`, `#greenChip`); in the Office `Office.open('finance', 'neighbours')`; `Settings.set('statsRange', 'months')` for the months; `S.stats.recent.length`, `S.stats.months.length`. The history must change no number of the game: `node arlanda-rising/tools/missions-check.js > a.txt` before and after a change to `sim/stats.js`, and `diff`.
  - The loans: `Game.startMission('1-6', 'medium'); Game.play(); Game.speed = 0; Game.openPanel('finance')` (the card at the top, Borrow in ops time); `Finance.limit()`, `Finance.room()`, `Finance.step()`, `Finance.cannot('borrow')`; a player who comes in broke: `S.money = 0.4e6; S.loans = 30e6` before playing 1-6 or 1-7 — a 5 MSEK loan buys the second fuel truck or two ploughs and keeps the money above zero (★★★ either way).
  - The mission's info from the pause menu: a mission played into a stage (as above), then `Game.pauseMenu(); Game.missionInfo()`; `Game.startChallenge('c1', 'medium'); Game.play(); Game.pauseMenu(); Game.missionInfo()` for a Challenge Day, `FreeSave.data = FreePlay.start('1976', 'medium'); Game.freeRound(); Game.play(); Game.pauseMenu(); Game.missionInfo()` for a Free Play round.
  - A turnaround at a moment: a mission played by its reference strategy (as the roofs-off recipe) until a flight at a stand matches, e.g. `f.tr.fuel === 'going' && S.clock < Turnaround.fuelStart(f.tr)` (the truck on its way; 1-6 and 1-9 fuel by hand, 1-2…1-5 and 1-7 the oil company's, 2-8 on the bags by hand, 5-8 de-icing, 8-1 the mobile charger, 8-3 the robots, 8-5 hydrogen), then `Game.speed = 0; const p = Traffic.pos(f, S.clock); Cam.centerOn(p.x, p.y, 3); FlightCard.open(f)` (people are drawn from zoom 1.3); `Turnaround.steps(f, S.clock)` is what the bar and the card show.
