# Arlanda Rising — the screens and the game in the browser

Part of the spec of `arlanda-rising`; the core rules and the index of these files are in [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md). Keep it in sync with the code like the rest of the spec. What each screen shows and does, then what `render/`, `ui/`, `game.js` and `progress.js` do today.

## Screens

- **Start screen:** the language switcher (EN · RU · SV), the title over a slow flyover of the player's current airport (or the 2040 Arlanda before a campaign exists), a button for each thing that can be continued, named for it — **Continue mission** (a mission interrupted: its name and stage; **Continue the Challenge Day** for a Challenge Day), **Continue campaign** (the chapter and the next mission), **Continue Free Play** (its year, its month and its money) — the first of them the big default; then **New Campaign**, **New Free Play** (asks first when a Free Play is saved) and **Challenge Days** when open, the difficulty (Easy / Medium / Hard with a line on what it changes), Sound On / Off, Settings (hints Full / Short / Off, graphics quality, sums in full or short — `1 200 000 SEK` or `1.2 MSEK`), the Archive and the Album.
- **Timeline (campaign map):** a horizontal timeline 1959 → 2040 with the chapters as eras, each with its missions as stops, their stars and minutes; locked eras greyed; a picture of the airport as it looked at the end of each finished chapter.
- **Office** (between missions; time stopped): the airport map in planning mode — the **master plan** with the next historical projects as outlined plots (their stages, pace, financing and options), the free plots, the terminals' insides, the advert spaces and their offers (see "The master plan" in [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md)); construction takes the calendar time that passes before the next mission — and the screens the Ops Center, Finance and Departments (one screen with tabs), Airlines with the **Offers** board and the Terminal view. A **"Meanwhile…"** report opens it: what happened in the weeks or months since the last mission (simulated in summary, see "Time" in [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md)), with the money, the meters and the news. The next mission's card is a tap away.
- **Mission card:** the year and season, the briefing in two or three speech bubbles by **Astrid** (the mentor, see "Hints" in [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md)), the goals and the three stars, the stages, the minutes, what is new in this mission (new buildings, systems, airlines, entities — each with a small picture and one line), **Start**.
- **Game view:** the isometric map (airside and landside). HUD at the top: money (opens Finance), date and clock, weather and forecast, the five meters (**Airlines, Passengers, Neighbours, Staff, Safety**), the stage's goals with progress, the speed buttons ⏸ / ▶ / ▶▶ / ▶▶▶ and **Skip to next event** (only when nothing manual is waiting). Left: the **task tray** (see "Hands" in [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md)). Bottom: **the bar** (`ui/toolbar.js`) — in ops time its live part (the desks, lanes and booths open with − / +, the ploughs to send, the runway direction, the teams short and the systems down), and always, in ops and plan time and in every kind of mission, **the buy menu** at its right end (`ui/buymenu.js`, see "The buy menu" below): 🛍️ **Services**, 🚜 **Vehicles**, 🏗️ **Build**. Big builds are the master plan's, in the plan panel and the Office. Overlays: delays, queues, noise contours over the towns, fire-service reach, snow on surfaces, stand occupancy, power and IT network.
- **The buy menu** (`ui/buymenu.js`, the categories in `data/buildings.js` `BUY_MENU`): every small build of the game is bought here, in ops and plan time, whatever the mission's kind — what changes is only what a mission has opened (see "What the campaign opens" in [chapters.md](chapters.md)). Three categories, each small build in exactly one (the checker fails otherwise): 🛍️ **Services** — what earns from the passengers and the tenants: kiosk, café, restaurant, duty-free shop, the two car parks, the airport coach stops, an office building, a hotel extension, the night train platform; 🚜 **Vehicles** — fuel truck, snowplough, de-icing truck, crash tender, mobile charger, electric apron vehicles; 🏗️ **Build** — remote stand, check-in desk, security lane, passport booth, cargo stand, charging stand, backup generator, solar park, security operations centre, hydrogen depot (RU *Сервис · Техника · Стройка*, SV *Service · Fordon · Bygg*). **The buttons:** at the bar's right end, an icon and the word (a green dot while the running mission opens something in it, until that category is opened); on a screen up to 600 px wide (a phone held upright) the bar has two rows — the live part above (ops time), the three buttons sharing the width below like tabs, so a desk's − / + stays whole. Keys **5**, **6**, **7** (beside the speeds 1–4). **The sheet:** a panel above the bar's right end (640 px, the cards in two columns) or, on a phone held upright, across the width above the bar (at most 66 % of the height; on its side it may cover the HUD). Its head has the three categories as tabs and ✕; ✕, **Esc**, the category's button again or a tap outside close it (a screen opened from it — Finance — keeps it open). It does **not** stop the time, as the bar's − / + do not: in ops time one buys while watching the queue; ⏸ or Space stops it. A focused button takes Space and Enter. The sheet's lines: Services — the departing passengers a day now and that the shops and car parks earn from each of them (*more passengers, more profit*), the shop cells free, the car parks' places against those needed; every category — *Ops time: what you buy is there at once* or *Plan time: each takes its days; what is still being built when the stage ends is finished then* (`Missions.endStage`). **A card:** the sign and the name, how many the airport has (of the most worth having, `Airport.cap`: *Fuel truck 4/8*, or ×n), **New** when the running mission opens it, what it does in a line (`about`), its price · when it is ready (ops time: at once; plan time: in its days, or *when this stage ends* when that comes first) · what it costs a month to run (`Invest.building` `out`: its jobs × `STAFF_MONTH`, `UPKEEP_YEAR` of its price, a lane's `LANE_MONTH`, a booth's `BOOTH_MONTH`) and its jobs; for what earns — **at today's traffic** (`Invest.paxDay`: the departing passengers a day of the last `TRAFFIC_DAYS` in summary; *No departing passengers yet* before the flights come): a shop's take (`SHOP_SPEND` × its `shop` share × 0.7 a departing passenger, the summary's own rule), a car park's (what its places add to the car parks' take, `Airport.parkingGain`: nothing once the places cover the drivers), the profit a month (take − running cost) and the payback (price ÷ profit, or *never pays back at today's traffic*); under it 📈 the same at **×2 passengers** (`Invest.at(2, …)`: the car parks' need grows with them too), so the player sees the profit grows with the traffic; a rent (offices, hotel wing, night train): the rent, *whatever the traffic*, with its profit and payback; the solar park: the energy it saves. **Every limit is on the card** (the audit of every kind: `Airport.cap`, the cells, the car parks' need, or none): the count in the name (*4/8*) and a dim line while there is room, how many of how many and why (`Text.capLine`: *4 of 8: one per stand (8 stands)* — fuel and de-icing trucks, mobile chargers; *4 of 8: a convoy of 4 per runway (2 runways)*; *2 of 3: each raises the fire category by one, up to 10*; *42 of 56: room for 14 more in the check-in halls*; *15 of 41: room for 26 more at the security checkpoints*; *6 of 12: room for 6 more in the passport hall*; *2 of 4: their row has room for 4*; *2 of 5: the cargo apron has room for 5*; *1 of 2: the green rating counts two*; *One is enough*; a charging stand *one on each stand that fits*; a shop *Takes 2 cells in a terminal · 3 of 6 free*; a car park *Car parks: 300 places, 700 needed*; the offices and the hotel wing *No limit*) and at the limit a full-strength amber line with the reason (never a faded button) and its explanation in full under it (`Text.buyTip`: a phone has no tooltips); what it needs first (`Airport.needs`, `Text.needWhy`: a terminal with a check-in hall, the international terminal, a security checkpoint, de-icing at the airport, passport control, the green rating, Cargo City) is such a line too. When the money does not cover it: *Not enough money: 3 MSEK short* and **💰 Borrow in Finance** beside **Buy** (Finance opens with its loans on top; a buy may still take the money below zero). **Buy · 600 kSEK** is green and buys at once, one tap (there was a second tap to confirm: twenty desks took forty taps; selling a shop on the terminal's card keeps its second tap, `SELL_CONFIRM_MS`); what is bought one by one in numbers (`bulk` in `data/buildings.js`: check-in desks, security lanes, passport booths, fuel trucks, ploughs, de-icing trucks, charging stands, mobile chargers) has **+5 · 750 k** and **+10 · 1.5 M** beside it (`BUY_BULK`), each shown while the limit leaves room for it, and the rest up to the limit when fewer than ten are left (*+4* for the last passport booths) and disabled while the money does not cover its total — each is that many single buys of the sim (`Actions.buyMany` → `Actions.buy`, the same checks and prices, stopping at the limit), the banner *Bought: Check-in desk ×5*. Under the cards, **Later**: 🔒 each thing a later mission opens — *Opens in mission 2-6: Fire Category* — or a later year (*From 2008*), in the order they open. The sim refuses what the menu shows locked or at its limit (`Actions.buy` → `Airport.why`); a decision's option still builds through `Airport.buy`, past the locks.

## The game in the browser (session 2)

What `render/`, `ui/`, `game.js` and `progress.js` do today; the screens of "Screens" not listed here come with the later sessions.

- **The map** (`render/`): isometric 2:1, north to the upper right, the viewer in the south-east; one world in metres (`data/layout.js`), drawn live every frame with no cached layer but the forest's texture. The forest is a texture from afar and single pines, spruces and birches from `ZOOM_TREES` on (bare birches in winter and April); fields, lakes (ice in winter), granite, Märsta's red and white houses and its church, the E4. The site project shows as it goes: the runway strip cleared from the south, the bog drying, the apron plot cleared from the west, the road in as gravel. Runway 1 with its markings, designators, snow (swept behind the convoy), cables being laid, edge, threshold and approach lights; the parallel taxiway and its links; the apron with its stands (lit green or red while a stand is picked, red when closed) and the remote stands bought later, each paved on its own (`LAYOUT.remote`) with a paved way straight to the apron's edge (and onto it when the stand's entry lies west of the apron, `AirportArt.remoteWay`) along the line the aircraft taxi in and out. No tree stands on what is built: the strip, the road and the link from the taxiway to the apron keep a corridor wide enough for the trees in front not to stand over them, and a remote stand, its way and every building with a plot in `data/layout.js` (the car parks east of the road in, by the bus stop; the café…) clear the forest round them (`render/map.js` `clearings`); so do, from the moment their work starts, the later aprons (`AirportArt.APRONS`), runways 2 and 3 with their strips, taxiways 2 and 3, the de-icing pads, Cargo City's yard and the solar parks. The trees are drawn whole, the crown's tip included. Buildings rise by their stages: the terminal's slab and walls, the waiting room (north of the hall, behind it as seen: drawn before it), the kiosk; the tower's foundations, shaft, cab and antenna; cranes over the stages being built — a tower crane (its mast, the jib swinging round, the counterweight, the driver's cab, the hook) beside the building, sorted at the building's south edge so that it is drawn in front of its walls and its jib over its roof. The first terminal's ARLANDA sign over the landside door has a small lamp at each end, never over the letters. Aircraft are drawn to scale from `data/aircraft.js` with the shape of each type (`AIRCRAFT_SHAPE`: sweep, jet pods or propellers, rear engines), a layered fuselage that shows the cheatline, the fin with the airline's emblem (`art/emblems.js`, the livery of the year from `data/airlines.js`; made smaller where the fin is narrower than the letters), a shadow that fades as they climb, and their lights. `render/traffic.js` turns the sim's times into motion, drawn at the sim's clock plus the part of the next step already run (`Sim.clockNow`), so the aircraft and the vehicles move every frame and not once a step: the approach (a holding circle when the runway is not free), the flare and the rollout, the taxi to the stand (on runway 1 the arrivals come onto the apron by the middle link and the departures leave by their own link at its north end, `LAYOUT.taxi.apronOut`, along a lane `outLane` m west of the arrivals' — one way in, one way out, never head-on), the turnaround (stairs — a second pair behind the wing of a wide-body of WIDE_SEATS seats or more —, passengers walking off and on as a stream of one figure for every PAX_PER_FIGURE of them, at least FIGURES_MIN and at most FIGURES_MAX a door (a DC-3 a few, a 747 two streams of 33; visual constants in `render/traffic.js`), boarding at its own pace, catering while the services run, baggage carts while they work (the carts sent by hand only from when they are sent), the fuel truck from its depot to the lane behind the stand, along it to the wing's side and up to the wing beside the fuselage, never under an aircraft, there while the fuel flows and back the same way — the oil company's blue-cabbed truck and the hydrogen bowser driving in as the passengers get off, an apron robot's low cart, the mobile charger's green van, a charging stand's cable to the nose; the tug), the pushback, the taxi out, the take-off roll and the climb; the plough convoy in echelon on the runway being swept, one plough on the apron while it is swept and the rest at the shed — each plough the airport owns drawn once; the trucks at their depot. Every ground vehicle (`render/vehicles.js`) is built of a few simple solids that say what it is at a glance — wheels, a chassis, a cab with its windscreen and the one telling part: the fuel truck a red cab and a round silver tank, the plough an orange truck pushing a wide yellow blade at an angle, the stair truck its stairs with steps and rails up to a platform over the cab, the catering truck a white box on its lift, the baggage tractor and its carts with bags, the de-icer its boom and basket, the low yellow tug with its tow bar, the red crash tenders with a water cannon, the white coaches with a band of windows, the site's lorries. Over each aircraft its flight number and, at a stand, a little Gantt bar (off · services · fuel · boarding, and the de-icing; see "The turnaround's bar and card" in [sim.md](sim.md)). The light follows `Calendar.sunElevation`: dawn and dusk tint, the dark, fog, falling snow, then the lamps glowing through the dark (sodium floodlights, lit windows, the tower's cab, beacons).
- **Five small additions to the sim for the map** (none changes a result): a flight keeps `rdir` (the runway direction it used), `airborne` (when it rolled) and `divertedAt`; a surface keeps `ploughFrom`; a turnaround keeps `fuelFrom` and `fuelAt` (the vehicle at the wing).
- **Play**: the HUD (money, the date and clock, the weather, the five meters, ⏸ ▶ ▶▶ ▶▶▶, Skip, pause), the stage's goals live (in the plan panel in plan time), the task tray (a ring for each deadline; a tap does the obvious, **Pick** or a drag chooses the stand on the map), the toolbar (the desks open of those there are, ploughs, the runway direction — the wind's, or the player's when calm with what each flies over the town and which is quieter, the small builds with their limits, one tap each (once two)), the plan panel (each project's stages with Start / Fast, progress and what they wait for, its options, *The owner's grant pays half of it.* or *A partner builds it with its own money* where that applies — no switch: a project is paid from the cash —, the terminals' units let with **🏪 Terminal view** (`n of m units let`: a phone has no T key, and the Terminal view is where the units are let to brands in plan time), **Teams and systems** — only the ladder of the domains the mission's goals ask for (`level`, `manager`, `handsOff`) and the IT people's +1 when a goal asks for a system, the rest behind *Teams, fallback teams, IT and heads* with 👥 Departments and 🛠️ Ops Center, *No head — find one* and *Two candidates for head* with 👥 Departments (see [people.md](people.md)) —, the fee level, loans), the decision card (the option's effects worked out from its data: money, days, a crew, the meters), the flight card. Panels below the HUD start under its real height (`--hudH`), so a phone in portrait works too; there the money shows short and without its currency (`1.2 M`, `45 k`, `1,2 млн`) in a chip of one width, so the first row does not jump as the sum changes; the weather chip is left out there and the date loses its 📅 (the 🌿 chip of chapter 6 still fits beside it on a 360 px phone), so the first row stays one line with a long month (`сентября`, `september`) and snow falling — in plan time the stage's **Day d of n** that chip shows goes beside the date as `d/n`, over the line of how far the stage has gone. **Skip** (⏭️ or N) says *Skipped to the next task* in a banner only the first time on the device (`arlandaRising.settings.v1` `skipTold`); afterwards the time just jumps. Every stage starts at ▶, whatever speed the stage before ran at (⏸ and back within a stage keeps the speed). Under the money the mission's result so far (`Missions.result()`: its income less its costs, green or red), so the player sees whether the airport earns or spends; under the date a line of how far the stage has gone (the clock between its start and end, or its days); the goals' box of an ops stage says the stage's end and the time left ("Until 10:00: 1 h 45 min left"), and a goal of the whole mission (1-8's profit) is shown live under the stage's goals and in the plan panel (`Text.missionGoals`, from `Missions.liveTotal()`). The toolbar of plan time shows the shops' cells free (see "The airport's own shops" in [sim.md](sim.md)) and the car parks' places against those the drivers need (see "Car parks" in [sim.md](sim.md)), each shop and car park button with how many there are (×2); once the room is full the button says so and `Airport.buy` refuses one more (it would earn nothing).
- **Help**: Kaj's live hints (`ui/hints.js`, at most one per `HINT_GAP`, each `HINT_REPEAT` times; Settings: Full / Short / Off) and the guiding finger in `GUIDED_MISSIONS` until the player has acted twice. The mission hints 💡 1–3, the concept cards and the Debrief come later.
- **The flow** (`game.js`): start screen (over the airport of 1962, or the campaign's own) → timeline → mission card → the card before each stage (the first one with "Meanwhile…": the months skipped, the flights in summary, the money, the owner's loan) → play → the stage card (Continue, or Retry stage / Restart mission) → the results (stars, the goals with their values, the meters, the work done by hand and by others, a line from Astrid or Kaj; after the exam the front page of *Arlanda-Bladet*, a fictional local paper) → the Office (the campaign's money and loans — borrow or repay there —, the last six months' books, what the airport has, the meters) → the next mission. The "Meanwhile…" report sits on the first stage's card rather than in the Office, as the skipped time is simulated when the mission starts.
- **The campaign and the stars** (`progress.js`): `Progress` keeps the best stars per mission and difficulty (a cheated run saves none); `Campaign` keeps the airport after the last mission passed in it, its difficulty (the mission card's choice becomes it) and the next mission; the world a campaign mission started from is kept in `arlandaRising.snapshots.v1` (plain JSON for now, a few kB a mission in chapter 1) for replays, which never touch the campaign. A mission opened with no snapshot (after a new campaign) starts from the forest with the owner building what it requires.
- **The autosave**: `{ run, data: Missions.save() }` under `arlandaRising.mission.v1`, every `AUTOSAVE_SECONDS` of play, at every stage's end, in the pause menu and on `visibilitychange` / `pagehide`; **Continue mission** resumes it paused at that very moment with a line on the aim, or on the stage card when it was saved there.
