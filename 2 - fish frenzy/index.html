🐟 Fish Frenzy

You're a small fish in a boundless sea. Eat plankton and fish smaller than you, grow and climb the food chain — but watch out for anything bigger than you!

← → or phone tilt — turn. Ctrl (or ↑), tap-and-hold on screen — dash (uses stamina, helps you catch prey or escape a predator).

The sea is boundless — no edges except the sea floor. Near the bottom it's darker, food is plentier, and fish are smallest. The higher you go, the lighter the water and the bigger (and more dangerous) the competition.

Green outline — you can eat that fish. Red outline — it will eat you. Jellyfish (pink) aren't lethal but sting and shrink you — avoid them.

Become the sea's biggest predator — the Sea King! (you're invulnerable for the first few seconds after starting — blue glow)
