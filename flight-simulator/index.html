✈ Aeropilot

First-person view from the pilot's cockpit. Controls: ← → — turn the plane, ↑ ↓ — climb or descend.

Hold Ctrl to fire from the crosshair at the center of the screen and pop the balloons — each one popped earns you points.

Fly to the highlighted airport (check the hint at the bottom), reduce altitude and line up with the runway — a successful landing also earns points. Don't crash into the ground short of the runway!

On phone and tablet: tilt the device left/right and forward/back instead of the arrow keys, tap the screen instead of Ctrl.
