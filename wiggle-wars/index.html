Wiggle Wars

Bazookas, grenades and air strikes on a land that blows apart. Play one against one on one phone, against the computer — or win the campaign!

Difficulty

Sound

Two teams of worms take turns. On your turn walk, jump, aim and fire one weapon before the time runs out — then run for cover! A worm that falls into the water is gone. The last team standing wins.

Hold Fire longer to shoot further. The wind at the top carries bazooka shots and air strikes. Grenades bounce and go off when their fuse runs out (1–5 seconds). The dotted line shows where your last shot flew.

Crates fall from the sky: health, weapons and tools like the jetpack, the teleport, double damage and low gravity. Oil barrels and mines blow up. In sudden death the water rises after every turn.

Keyboard: ← → walk, ↑ ↓ aim, Space hold to fire, Enter jump, Backspace back flip, 1–5 the fuse, Q or Tab or the right mouse button the weapons, C the camera back to the worm, Esc pause. Click the map to pick a target, drag it to look around, the wheel zooms.

Phone and tablet: the game goes fullscreen. A joystick appears where you touch the left half: sideways to walk, up and down to aim. Hold Fire at the bottom right to shoot; Jump and Back flip are above it. Tap the map to pick a target, drag it to look around, pinch to zoom.
