Lift Logic

Be the brain of a lift! Build its program from blocks and watch a whole building live by it.

You are Lifty, the brain of a lift. People come, press the call buttons ▲ ▼ and ride. Your program decides where the lift goes. It knows only what a real lift knows — its buttons, its floor, its load — it doesn't see the people.

Building a program: tap a colour chip to see its blocks, tap a block to add it under the selected one. Tap a block in your program to move, copy, wrap or delete it; tap a white slot to type a number or to put a sensor block in it. ▶ Run starts the day.

Phone and tablet: everything is a tap; you can also drag blocks. Drag the empty canvas to look around, pinch to zoom, drag the bar with ▶ to give more room to the building or to the code.

Keyboard: Space run / stop, P pause, S step, 1–4 the speed, E the Lift's eyes, Delete deletes the selected block, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo, Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy / paste, Esc the menu.

Every mission has three stars: the goal, and two aims like short waits, little energy or few blocks. Stuck? Tap 💡 for a hint.
